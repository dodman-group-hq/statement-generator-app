Statement Generator ("the Service") lets an account holder ("you") upload a Word template and a spreadsheet of data, and receive back one personalized PDF per row - optionally password-protected with that row's own ID. The Service is provided by Dodman Group ("we", "us").

  • If you believe a key or your password has been exposed, contact us and we'll help you revoke the key or secure the account.

1. What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a one-way (bcrypt) hash of your password - never the password itself. For this data we act as the data controller.
  • API keys: a label you give each key and a one-way hash of the key. We never store a key in plain text, so we can verify it without being able to see it.
  • Files you upload: the Word template and spreadsheet you submit for a given job, and the PDF statements generated from them. These may contain personal data about the people named in your spreadsheet (names, ID numbers, and whatever else your template includes). For this data we act as a processor on your behalf.
  • Job metadata: when a job ran, how many rows it processed, and whether it succeeded - used for enforcing plan limits and for our own troubleshooting. This does not include the content of your files.
  • Session cookie: when you log in we set one strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in (up to 7 days). It isn't used for advertising or tracking.
  • In your browser: if you use the generator page, your API key is remembered in your own browser's local storage so you don't have to retype it. It's sent only to this service, and you can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.

2. How long we keep it

  • Uploaded templates and spreadsheets are deleted from disk as soon as processing finishes (success or failure) - they are never kept longer than the single job that used them.
  • Generated PDF statements are available for download for up to 2 hours after a job completes, after which they're automatically and permanently deleted.
  • Job metadata (row counts, timestamps, pass/fail status - not file content) is retained for usage tracking and billing history.
  • Account information and API key records are kept until you ask us to delete your account.

3. Where it's processed and stored

Files are processed on our application server (currently hosted on Render) and are not copied anywhere else. Account and job metadata is stored in a Postgres database (currently hosted on Neon). Each account's job records are isolated using database-level access controls (Row-Level Security), so one account's data cannot be read by a database query scoped to another account - even by mistake in our own code.

4. Who else sees it

We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. The only parties with any access are infrastructure providers that host the Service (currently Render for compute and Neon for the database), who process data solely to provide that hosting. If we turn on error tracking, error reports may be sent to our error-tracking provider (currently Sentry); they identify the failing job and account label, not file contents.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you or your data subjects are located (including under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and, for people in the European Economic Area, the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Given our short retention window for files, most requests are already satisfied automatically. To access, correct, or delete your account information, or to have job metadata removed early, email inquiries@dodman.group from the address on your account.

6. Security

  • Passwords and API keys are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text.
  • All traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Optional password protection lets you lock each generated PDF with that row's own ID.
  • Account data is isolated at the database level using Row-Level Security, not just application-level checks.

Questions or requests about your data: inquiries@dodman.group
